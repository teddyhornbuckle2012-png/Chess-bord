Geometric Chess - Bug Fix Verification

This page verifies the fixes for the chess game bugs.

Test Results

Manual Tests

Please verify these manually:

  1. Test A: Initial board
    • Load index.html in a browser
    • Confirm all 32 pieces appear correctly
    • Confirm board has correct orientation (cream at bottom, AI at top)
    • Confirm squares have correct colors (pale ivory and olive)
  2. Test B: New Game on fresh board
    • Load index.html
    • Click "New Game" button
    • Confirm standard starting position is displayed
    • Confirm human player moves first
    • Confirm AI does not move automatically
  3. Test C: New Game during active game
    • Make a legal human move
    • Allow AI to respond
    • Click "New Game"
    • Confirm initial position is restored
    • Confirm move history and captures are cleared
  4. Test D: New Game during AI thinking
    • Start a game and make a legal move
    • Click "New Game" while AI is calculating
    • Confirm new game starts immediately
    • Wait and confirm no move from old game appears
  5. Test E: Repeated restarts
    • Click "New Game" several times in succession
    • Confirm application remains responsive
    • Confirm no duplicate pieces or highlights
  6. Test F: Mobile touch input
    • Test on mobile or resize browser to mobile size
    • Tap "New Game" button
    • Confirm it responds without double tap
    • Confirm board remains correctly aligned
  7. Test G: Chess functionality
    • Select each piece type
    • Confirm legal destinations appear
    • Confirm illegal moves are rejected
    • Confirm AI responds after legal human move
